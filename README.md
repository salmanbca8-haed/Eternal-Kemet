# Eternal-Kemet
## Egypt: The Land of Eternal Wonder — Cinematic Scrollytelling Experience

A breathtaking, historically inspired Ancient Egypt exploration experience. Traverse the Giza plateau at dawn, witness the construction of the Great Pyramid, voyage along the life-giving Nile, and discover sacred temples through smooth scroll-driven frame animation.

Designed & Developed by **Salman**.

---

### Features

- **Scroll-Controlled Cinematic Animation:** 600-frame sequence rendered smoothly via HTML5 Canvas with lerp interpolation and dynamic preloading.
- **8 Historical Chapters:**
  1. *Scene 01:* The Land of Eternity (Giza at Dawn)
  2. *Scene 02:* The Sacred Nile (Source of Life)
  3. *Scene 03:* The Great Pyramid (A Vision Carved in Stone)
  4. *Scene 04:* The Hands That Shaped History (Builders & Community)
  5. *Scene 05:* Where Art Became Eternity (Hieroglyphs & Astronomy)
  6. *Scene 06:* Monuments to the Divine (Hypostyle Halls & Crypts)
  7. *Scene 07:* An Ancient Soul. A Living World (Sphinx & Sahara)
  8. *Scene 08:* Some Wonders Are Never Forgotten (Climax & Legacy)
- **Authentic Egyptian Visual Identity:** Obsidian Black, Ancient Sandstone, Weathered Papyrus, Deep Nile Blue, and Muted Ivory.
- **Audio Atmosphere:** Integrated atmospheric Egyptian soundtrack with smooth volume fading and animated equalizer wave bars.
- **Interactive Modals:**
  - High-resolution deep-zoom photography for Dendera Temple and Royal Crypt.
  - Full-screen cinema player for the Great Pyramid Construction film.
